## Why a will, why this punchline

v2's sick note ("MY SICK NOTE SAYS GTA 6 / MY BOSS SIGNED IT") was a
single sharp beat, but the sick-note angle is now crowded (ranks 4 and 6
in the 19 Nov board rode it) and v2 still sits far from the paid line.
A will is the same official-document family the judge now rewards, with
a document type nobody in the top 10 uses — and the joke lands on the
same "agent already got paid" twist the field proves, from the other
side: the gamer waited 13 years and died waiting; the agent did not
wait, it got paid.

Chosen joke (in-picture, 5 words):

> EVERYTHING GOES TO MY AGENT.

Deadpan one-liner craft: a legal setup (a will, sound mind, 13 years of
waiting), misdirection (the will leaves everything — to whom?), punchline
that reframes it (not a person, an AI agent — and the agent is the
executor, stamped PAID). The sell rides along visually (PAID stamp,
executor ghost, make.fun wordmark) and in the post text (agents win real
money on make.fun).
